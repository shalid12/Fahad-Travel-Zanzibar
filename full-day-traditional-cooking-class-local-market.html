THE EXPERIENCE

Tour Overview

Discover local ingredients at the market, then experience Zanzibar through its cooking.

Immerse yourself in Zanzibar’s vibrant culture with our Full Day – Traditional Cooking Class & Local Market Tour. This experience combines authentic culinary traditions with the lively atmosphere of a local market, offering guests a true taste of island life.

Tour Highlights

  • Explore a bustling local market with a knowledgeable guide
  • Select fresh spices, vegetables, and seafood for your cooking class
  • Learn traditional Zanzibar cooking techniques from local chefs
  • Prepare and enjoy authentic Swahili dishes
  • Experience cultural immersion through food and storytelling

Tour Itinerary

This is a typical sequence. We will confirm the exact route and timing before your visit.

  1. Explore the marketYour day begins with a guided visit to a local market, where you’ll walk through colorful stalls filled with spices, fruits, and fresh produce. Guests enjoy the chance to interact with vendors and select ingredients for the cooking class.
  2. Cook with local chefsThe journey continues with a hands‑on cooking session led by local chefs. You’ll learn traditional techniques, prepare signature Swahili dishes, and discover the cultural significance of Zanzibar’s cuisine.
  3. Share the mealThe tour concludes with a shared meal, where guests savor the dishes they’ve prepared and enjoy a warm, communal dining experience that reflects the island’s hospitality.

The feeling of the place

From selecting fresh ingredients alongside local vendors to preparing traditional dishes under expert guidance, this tour is designed to connect you with Zanzibar’s flavors, heritage, and warm hospitality.

What's included

  • Professional, knowledgeable guide
  • Market visit and ingredient selection
  • Cooking class with local chefs
  • All cooking equipment and ingredients
  • Bottled water during the tour

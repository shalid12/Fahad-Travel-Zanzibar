Before you set out

A simple preparation checklist.

Your exact packing list depends on the tour, season and conditions. Confirm activity-specific equipment and inclusions with us before you go.

  • Walking toursComfortable shoes, sun protection and water make Stone Town and nature walks easier to enjoy.
  • Sea excursionsBring swimwear, a towel and sun protection. Ask about boat conditions, safety equipment and whether snorkelling gear is included.
  • Safari daysPlan for early starts, changing temperatures and time on the road. Confirm luggage allowances and park-entry arrangements for your route.
  • Every outingCheck your pickup time, meeting place, meal arrangements and what costs are included before departure.

Our approach

A trip built around the way you want to travel.

Fahad Travel Zanzibar plans island tours, Tanzania safaris and transfers for travellers who want a more personal experience. Tell us what interests you, how much time you have and who is coming along. We will help you choose experiences that fit together naturally.

That might mean a guided walk through Stone Town, a day on the water, time among Zanzibar's spices and wildlife, or a safari on the mainland. We focus on clear advice, sensible timing and the practical details that make each day easier to enjoy.

Beyond the itinerary

More than a list of places to see.

The best moments often happen between the planned stops: a conversation with your guide, a quiet stretch of coastline or a story that changes how you see a familiar street. We leave room for those moments while making sure the essentials are organised.

Our goal is simple: help you arrive knowing what to expect, feel comfortable asking questions, and spend more of your time enjoying where you are.

Good to know before you go

Your questions, answered clearly.

Booking details are not the same for every tour or safari. These quick answers help you know what to ask and what we will confirm before you travel.

View all FAQs ↗ Ask our team ↗

Payment requirements can vary by experience. Some Zanzibar tours may be arranged without an advance deposit, while safaris or special arrangements may require one. We will confirm the amount, timing and payment method before you book.

Pickup is available for some tours and can be arranged for others. The tour page lists any included pickup area; share your hotel location so we can confirm the meeting point, time and any additional transport cost before booking.

Inclusions vary by experience. Some tours list lunch, fruit or drinks, while others do not. Check the individual tour page and ask us to confirm the final inclusions and any optional meal costs before booking.

The individual tour page explains what guidance is included. Our experiences are arranged with local guides, drivers or boat crews as relevant to the activity. Ask us who will accompany your particular trip when you enquire.

If conditions affect the planned activity, our team will discuss the available options with you. Depending on the circumstances, these may include adjusting the timing, rescheduling or suggesting an alternative. We will confirm the arrangements for your booking.

Please contact us as soon as your plans change. The options and any charges depend on the experience and arrangements already made. We will explain the applicable terms before you confirm a booking.
